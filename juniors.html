ALS Juniors

Little Readers. Big Imaginations.

Every lifelong reader begins somewhere. ALS Juniors is our initiative to introduce children to books, stories, language, imagination and creative expression.

We believe reading should begin with curiosity and pleasure, not pressure. Through interactive storytelling, age-appropriate reading activities, creative exercises and conversations, we hope to help children build a meaningful relationship with literature.

What ALS Juniors Offers

Built for Curious Young Minds

Storytelling Sessions

Encouraging imagination and attentive listening.

Reading Circles

Helping children explore books together.

Creative Writing

Activities that allow young minds to express themselves.

Author Interactions

Introducing children to the people behind their favourite stories.

Literary Games & Workshops

Making language enjoyable.

Share Their Own Stories

Opportunities for young readers to share their own stories and poems.

For Parents

Give Your Child a World Beyond the Screen.

Books offer children something that cannot be measured only through marks or examinations. They develop imagination, empathy, language, concentration and the ability to understand different perspectives. ALS Juniors hopes to make reading a joyful part of childhood.
