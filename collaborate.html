Let's Create Something Meaningful Together.

Literature grows when communities, institutions and individuals come together. Ahmedabad Literary Society welcomes opportunities to collaborate with schools, colleges, universities, libraries, bookstores, cultural organisations, publishers and independent literary initiatives.
