Join ALS

Your Next Favourite Conversation Might Begin Here.

You do not have to be a writer to join Ahmedabad Literary Society. You do not need an impressive reading list, a degree in literature, or an opinion about every book. You only need curiosity, openness and a willingness to participate.

Whether you read every day or are trying to return to reading after years, ALS welcomes people who believe in the value of stories and conversations.

Why Become Part of ALS?

Membership offers an opportunity to connect with fellow readers and writers, participate in literary gatherings, discover books and authors, and contribute to a growing cultural community. We encourage members to participate actively, listen respectfully and help create a welcoming environment for others.

What We Ask of Every Member
