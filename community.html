Our Community

The People Behind the Pages

Every literary community is shaped by the people who participate in it. At ALS, our strength lies in the diversity of our readers, writers, poets, educators and volunteers. Some come to share their work. Others come to listen. Many arrive as strangers and gradually become part of a community connected by books.
