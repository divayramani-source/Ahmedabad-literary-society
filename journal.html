The Literary Journal

Reading. Writing. Questioning. Remembering.

A space for words that deserve more than a passing moment on social media. The ALS Literary Journal brings together original poetry, essays, book reviews, author interviews, literary reflections and creative writing from our community.

Submission Guidelines

Do you have a poem, essay, short story, book review or literary reflection you would like to share? The ALS Literary Journal welcomes original contributions from writers and readers. Submissions are reviewed for originality, clarity, literary merit and relevance. Publication is subject to editorial selection.

Submissions are emailed directly to Ahmedabadliterarysociety@gmail.com.
