About Us

Our Story

Ahmedabad Literary Society was founded in 2025 with a simple thought: literature deserves a community where people can experience it together.

Our earliest gatherings were intimate conversations among people who loved reading. Our first book discussion, centred on Calling of the Heart by Ishmeet Kaur Chaudhary, marked the beginning of a journey that would gradually bring together readers, writers, poets, educators and literary enthusiasts across Ahmedabad.

Over time, ALS has grown beyond traditional book discussions. We have hosted poetry gatherings, author interactions, storytelling sessions, workshops, literary celebrations and collaborative cultural programmes.

What remains unchanged is our belief that literature becomes richer when it is shared. ALS continues to grow through the participation, commitment and enthusiasm of its community.

Our Vision

To nurture an inclusive, intellectually vibrant literary culture where reading, writing, creativity, and critical thinking become meaningful parts of everyday life.

Our Mission
  • 01Encourage reading across generations and languages.
  • 02Create meaningful opportunities for emerging and established writers.
  • 03Provide platforms for poetry, storytelling, dialogue and creative expression.
  • 04Promote critical thinking, cultural understanding and freedom of expression.
  • 05Introduce children and young adults to the joy of literature.
  • 06Build collaborations that make literature more accessible to communities.
What We Believe

Literature Belongs to Everyone.

A person does not need to be a published writer, a scholar, or an expert reader to participate in a literary conversation. We value curiosity over credentials, dialogue over agreement, and participation over hierarchy.

We welcome different languages, experiences, opinions and forms of expression. We believe respectful disagreement can enrich a discussion, and that literature has the power to challenge assumptions without diminishing human dignity.

A Note From the Founder

A Community Built Around the Love of Words

When I started Ahmedabad Literary Society, I wanted to create a space where people could come together because they loved books, not because they needed an introduction, a designation, or a particular literary background.

I have always believed that a book becomes more meaningful when we discuss it. Sometimes another reader notices something we missed. Sometimes a poem gives words to a feeling we have carried for years. And sometimes a conversation changes the way we look at the world. ALS grew from that belief.

Today, what gives me the greatest satisfaction is seeing people discover their own voices, share their writing, listen to one another, and develop friendships through literature. I want ALS to remain a space where an established author and a first-time reader can sit together and feel equally welcome.

There is much more we hope to do — more books to read, more voices to discover, more children to introduce to stories, and more meaningful conversations to begin. And I hope we never lose the warmth with which this journey started.

Shraddha Ahuja Ramani
Founder & Director, Ahmedabad Literary Society

About the Founder

Shraddha Ahuja Ramani

Founder & Director, Ahmedabad Literary Society | Founder, Savitri Publications

Shraddha Ahuja Ramani is an author, poet, educationist, literary curator, women's rights activist and social rights advocate based in Ahmedabad. With over two decades of experience in education and multiple postgraduate degrees in diverse disciplines, she brings an interdisciplinary perspective to literature, education and social issues.

An author of several books, her writing explores gender, identity, society, human relationships and social justice. She is also the founder of Savitri Publications, an independent publishing house dedicated to nurturing literary voices.

As the Founder and Director of Ahmedabad Literary Society, she is committed to making literature accessible, encouraging meaningful dialogue, promoting freedom of expression and building a community where diverse voices are heard and valued.
