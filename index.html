Who We Are

Not Just a Book Club. A Literary Community.

Ahmedabad Literary Society is an independent literary community dedicated to bringing literature closer to people. What began as a shared love for books has grown into a space where readers meet writers, poetry finds an audience, ideas are discussed, and stories become conversations.

We believe literature should not remain confined to bookshelves, academic institutions, or literary festivals. It should be part of everyday life. At ALS, every reader has a place, every thoughtful question matters, and every voice deserves to be heard.
